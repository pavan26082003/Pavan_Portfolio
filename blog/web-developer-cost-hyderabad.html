How Much Does It Cost to Hire a Web Developer in Hyderabad? (2026 Price Guide)

Pavan Kiran Nagapuri March 10, 2026 5 min read

One of the most searched questions in Hyderabad's business community: "How much does a website cost?" The honest answer is: it depends. But vague answers don't help you budget. This guide breaks down real 2026 prices for hiring a web developer in Hyderabad — from a simple portfolio to a full-stack application.

Freelancer vs Agency — What's the Difference?

Before looking at prices, understand who you're hiring:

  • Freelance developer: You work directly with one developer. Lower cost, direct communication, faster turnaround. Best for most small businesses and startups.
  • Web agency: A team with project managers, designers, and developers. Higher cost (you're paying for overhead), but better for large, complex projects with ongoing support contracts.
  • Freelance platforms (Fiverr, Upwork): Cheapest option, but quality is inconsistent and communication can be difficult. Good for very simple tasks, risky for full projects.

For most businesses in Hyderabad — startups, real estate agencies, small businesses — a local freelance developer gives the best value. You get quality work, direct communication in Telugu/Hindi/English, and someone who understands the local market.

Simple Site vs Full Stack App — Price Ranges

Here's a realistic breakdown of web development costs in Hyderabad in 2026:

Simple Portfolio / Landing Page — ₹15,000–₹25,000

  • 5–7 sections (hero, about, services, contact)
  • Mobile-responsive design
  • Contact form with email notification
  • Basic SEO setup
  • Delivered in 1–2 weeks

Business Website — ₹25,000–₹45,000

  • 10–15 pages (home, about, services, blog, contact)
  • Custom design with your branding
  • WhatsApp integration, Google Maps
  • On-page SEO for local keywords
  • Delivered in 2–4 weeks

E-commerce Website — ₹40,000–₹70,000

  • Product listings with cart and checkout
  • Payment gateway integration (Razorpay/PayU)
  • Order management
  • Mobile-first design
  • Delivered in 4–6 weeks

Full Stack Web Application — ₹60,000–₹1,20,000+

  • Custom React frontend + Django/Node backend
  • User authentication, database, REST API
  • Admin dashboard
  • Deployment on cloud (Render, AWS, etc.)
  • Delivered in 6–12 weeks

What Affects the Cost?

Five factors drive the price up or down:

  1. Number of pages/features — more pages = more time = higher cost
  2. Custom design vs template — a fully custom design costs 30–50% more than a template-based site
  3. Backend complexity — a static site is cheap; a site with user logins, databases, and APIs is significantly more
  4. Timeline — rush projects (under 1 week) typically cost 20–30% more
  5. Ongoing maintenance — some developers charge a monthly retainer (₹2,000–₹8,000/month) for updates and support
